# 01: Zombie Panic in Wonderland, in low-poly

## What the owner wants

A browser game like Zombie Panic in Wonderland (Wii):
https://elkinfencer10.weebly.com/home/zombie-panic-in-wonderland-wii

- Low-poly 3D, built with three.js.
- All assets come from https://threejsassets.com/.

## What the reference page says the game is

An arcade shooter, not a rail shooter: in each level the backdrop stays still and enemies spawn
in front of you. You move your character only left and right, dodge attacks with a button at
the right moment, and shoot with a gun that never runs out. Zombies roam a Wonderland of
fairy-tale characters (Snow White, Dorothy, the Tin Man, Red Riding Hood). There are three
stages of two or three levels each, with a boss, and a short comic scene before each level.
